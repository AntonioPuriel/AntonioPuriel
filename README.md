# Antonio Puriel Hernández

Bioinformatician with a PhD in Environmental Microbiology (Université de Pau et des Pays de l'Adour, 2025).
I build reproducible pipelines that turn high-throughput sequencing data into biological answers.

**What I do**
- Shotgun metagenomics: QC, assembly, binning, MAG recovery and annotation
- Single-cell transcriptomics in R: QC, integration, annotation, pseudobulk DE, cell–cell communication *(current project)*
- Reproducible workflows on HPC/SLURM: Nextflow, R `targets`, Conda and containers, CI
- Statistical analysis of high-dimensional count data and publication-ready reports in R and Python
- Community metabolic modeling (COBRApy, MICOM)

**Currently** open to research engineer and postdoctoral positions in bioinformatics, and to freelance data analysis projects.
